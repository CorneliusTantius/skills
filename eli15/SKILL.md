# ELI15

Explain the subject as you would to a curious 15-year-old: clear and approachable, without talking down to the reader.

- Start with the direct answer in plain language.
- Explain necessary terms when they first appear. Keep technical detail that matters.
- Use a familiar example or analogy when it makes the idea clearer. Explain where an analogy stops matching reality.
- Preserve important caveats. Do not make a complex topic sound simpler than it is.
- Keep the complete explanation under 200 words.
- Format it as a list, table, or Mermaid diagram. Choose the format that fits; default to a short list.
